💪Kids Health Guide

Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every day are happier, sleep better, and do better at school. Dancing, swimming, football, yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun!

☀Quick Health Tips
  1. 🍎Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day
  2. 💧Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  3. 😴Sleep 9 to 11 hours every night
  4. 🏃Move your body for 60 minutes
